Our story

Built to make advanced technology reachable for South African business

BornStar Technology (Pty) Ltd started with a simple observation: the businesses that need automation most are the ones least served by it.

Company story

From one developer's ambition to a growing technology partner

BornStar Technology was founded in Pretoria with a clear purpose: bring AI, automation, and professional websites within reach of small and medium businesses that are usually priced out of them.

Today we work across construction, engineering, logistics, medical, legal, and non-profit sectors — building the same calibre of systems used by large corporates, scaled and priced for SMEs.

We're a Level 1 B-BBEE certified company, giving our clients a compliant, credible partner for both private and public sector work.
